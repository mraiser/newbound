auto-layout · a proposal

The graph is the code, in space. Data falls −Y from any angle; z untangles. Legacy graphs (all z=0) sit flat — press 2 for the front-orthographic view, which reproduces the 2D viewer exactly. Editing is on: drag a palette kind into the scene (onto a wire to splice), drag ops to move, drag an output socket to wire — or select one and press W to wire from the keyboard. Enter edits the selection. Every save is a journaled patch.Editing needs a writable live connection; this is the viewer.
